(1963) AW Bug


The AW Bug originated in pre-WW2 times as a vehicle for the average citizen. As one of AW’s earliest models, it helped establish Automobilwerke Wolfsburg as a leading manufacturer of practical, accessible cars.

In the 1950s and 60s, it succeeded worldwide due to its simple, reliable design. Its distinctive shape and engineering made it instantly recognizable and beloved by many. Its iconic silhouette and enduring charm have led to appearances in numerous films, cementing the Bug as both a cultural and automotive classic.
